`get_card_for_birthdate` returns **five** Western/sidereal sign blocks under `astro_wheels`. They are **different systems**, not five copies of one answer. On many dates the names match; on others they diverge — that is expected.

## The five wheels

| Key in JSON | What it is | Signs |
|-------------|------------|-------|
| `tropical` | Season-anchored (vernal equinox = 0°). Common “Western” magazine horoscope frame | 12 |
| `sidereal_true` | True sidereal / capital-star wheel (GEOMETRY’s 13-sign frame, includes **Ophiuchus**) | 13 |
| `sidereal_iau` | IAU constellation boundaries along the ecliptic (unequal spans; includes **Ophiuchus**) | 13 |
| `fagan_bradley` | Western sidereal with **Fagan-Bradley** ayanamsa | 12 |
| `vedic_rasi` | Vedic rāśi with **Lahiri**-style sidereal frame (+ nested nakshatra where returned) | 12 |

Always read **`sign_name`** (and `system_slug`) on the wheel you care about. Do not treat `sign_no` as a universal zodiac index — see [Field glossary](/field-glossary).

## “Wrong sign” is usually the wrong wheel

If a newspaper or app says “Pisces” and `astro_wheels.sidereal_true.sign_name` says something else (or Ophiuchus), you are comparing **different anchors**, not catching a GEOMETRY bug.

**Self-check before filing anything:**

1. Which wheel does your source use (tropical vs sidereal vs Vedic)?  
2. Open the matching key under `astro_wheels` in the raw JSON.  
3. For sky dates (moon / season / retro), also read [Calendar boundaries](/calendar-boundaries) — GMT day vs local almanac is a separate class of “off by one.”

## Coverage

All five wheels ship on the published **1900–2100** focus window. See [Data coverage](/coverage).
