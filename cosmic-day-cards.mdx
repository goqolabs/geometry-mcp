Each Gregorian **month + day** maps to exactly one **cosmic** playing-card face (366 faces — year does not matter). [`get_date`](/tools/get-date) returns that face as the `cosmic` block for the date you pass. Traditional cardology still calls this the **birth card**; on the wire it is just the date’s cosmic face.

This page is the full calendar grid: **rows = day of month (1–31)**, **columns = month**. Empty cells (`—`) are days that do not exist in that month (for example April 31).

## Special days

| Date | Card | Notes |
|------|------|--------|
| **February 29** | **9♣** | Leap-day cosmic face |
| **December 31** | **W★** | Joker / wild card |

All other days are the usual 52 playing cards (A–K × ♠♦♣♥).

**How to read a cell:** find the **day** on the left, then the **month** column. Example: March 15 → **8♦** (same face [`get_date`](/tools/get-date) returns for `1985-03-15`).

**Tradition:** This month/day → playing-card map is public **cardology** (also called Cards of Destiny / Science of the Cards). The classic **birth-card** chart was set out for a general audience in Edith L. Randall and Florence Evylinn Campbell, *Sacred Symbols of the Ancients* (1947), following Olney H. Richmond, *The Mystic Test Book* (1893) — [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/mystictestbookor00rich). Later popular books (for example Robert Lee Camp, *Cards of Your Destiny*) teach the same calendar. Published here so builders do not have to hunt scattered sites for the grid.
